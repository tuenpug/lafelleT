How to climb

Hold Q / E / A / D: the suggested hold turns red. Let go of the key to move there.
+ arrow keys: move the red marker to another hold first.
Swing 3 times (tap ↑↓ or ←→): a hand can dyno 2–3× further than a normal reach (orange hold). The whole body flies — then put 2 more limbs on fast!
⚡ Energy drains on every hold. Q or E + Space shakes out and chalks that arm. Rest on big jugs with both feet on.
🔗 Rope: every 5 moves she clips the next quickdraw. Fall, and the rope catches her at the last clip and lowers her down.
Put a hand on TOP and hold it for a second to finish. One limb per hold!
Feet still on the mat? Press ↓ to step back and choose another start (← → walk, ↑ step up).
Hanging on one hand / off balance? You can't let go or make another big move: place your other limbs first.
